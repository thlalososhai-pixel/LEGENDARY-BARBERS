Our story

Three barbers, one bench of tools passed down twice already

Legendary Barbers started in a single-chair studio behind a cabinet maker's shop — which is why the name stuck. We still cut the way that first shop taught us: unhurried, precise, and finished with a hot towel rather than a rush out the door.

Walk-ins are welcome when a chair is free, but booking ahead guarantees your barber and your slot.
